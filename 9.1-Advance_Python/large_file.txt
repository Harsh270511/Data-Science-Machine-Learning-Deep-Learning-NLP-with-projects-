The Mauryan Empire was one of the largest and most powerful empires in ancient India. It was founded by Chandragupta Maurya around 322 BCE with the guidance of Chanakya (Kautilya). The empire reached its greatest extent under Emperor Ashoka, who is famous for spreading Buddhism after the Kalinga War. The Mauryan rulers developed a strong administration, army, economy, and system of governance. The empire played an important role in shaping the political and cultural history of ancient India.
